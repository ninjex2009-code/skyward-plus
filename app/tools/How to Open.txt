How to open Skyward Plus
========================

Skyward Plus is not signed with a paid Apple developer account, so the
first time you open it your Mac says Apple can't check it for malware.
That is expected. You only have to allow it once.

1. Drag "Skyward Plus" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Terminal (press Command-Space, type Terminal, press Return),
   paste this line and press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Skyward Plus.app"

   Then open Skyward Plus from Applications as usual.

   Instead of Terminal you can double-click "Allow Skyward Plus.command"
   in this window. If your Mac blocks that too, use the Terminal line.

Other way, without Terminal
---------------------------
1. Open Skyward Plus from Applications. Click "Done" on the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Skyward Plus. Enter your Mac password.
3. Open Skyward Plus again and click "Open Anyway".

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Your Skyward password stays on your Mac, in its Keychain, and is only
sent to your school district's own sign-in page.
