# Product

## Register

product

## Users
High school students who check their grades in Skyward many times a week, on a Mac and an iPhone, often between classes or at night while doing homework. They want to know quickly whether anything changed, what is missing or due, and how each class is going, without fighting the district portal.

## Product Purpose
Skyward Plus reads a student's Skyward grades, assignments, schedule and attendance and presents them in a fast, friendly native app with alerts, a planner and a small cloud mascot named Nimbus. Success is a student opening it, understanding where they stand in a few seconds, and feeling calmer rather than more anxious.

## Brand Personality
Cozy, playful, calm. Warm and encouraging rather than judgmental; personality comes from Nimbus, the living background worlds and small moments of delight, while the grade data itself stays clear and honest.

## Anti-references
- Skyward itself: dated, gray, cramped school-portal tables.
- Generic AI dashboards: glass cards everywhere, big-number stat tiles with tiny labels, gradient accents, identical card grids.

## Design Principles
1. Calm first: lead with what changed and what needs you; never make a grade feel louder than it is.
2. Warmth through character, clarity through data: playfulness lives in Nimbus, the worlds and motion, not in the numbers.
3. Native, not templated: use the platform's own materials and controls, shaped with intent rather than defaults.
4. Every surface earns its card: group only when grouping helps; prefer rhythm and type over boxes.
5. Delight is a reward, not decoration: celebrations and sounds respond to real events.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for text, Reduce Motion respected everywhere (living backgrounds and Nimbus pause or simplify), VoiceOver labels on all controls, Dynamic Type where text is read.
